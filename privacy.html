Inkwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Inkwell is a personal journaling app for Android and the web. It is built so that nobody except you can read what you write. That includes the people who make it.

What Inkwell stores, and where

  • On your phone: your entries, moods, photos and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you turn on sync): encrypted copies of your entries go in Drive's hidden app data folder. Photos are not synced.
  • Nowhere else. Inkwell has no servers of its own. It collects no analytics, shows no ads and sells no data.

Encryption

Before an entry leaves your device, it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key that encrypts it is derived from a passphrase that only you know. Google Drive stores only the encrypted data, plus timestamps that tell the app which entries changed. Neither Google nor the makers of Inkwell can read your entries. The flip side: if you forget your passphrase and lose your recovery key, nobody can recover your synced entries.

Google user data

Inkwell asks for one Google permission, drive.appdata. That lets it see, create and delete only its own files in a hidden folder of your Google Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files. Inkwell uses this access only to store and sync your encrypted journal. It doesn't transfer this data to anyone else, doesn't use it for advertising, and doesn't let humans read it. Inkwell's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI features

  • On-device AI: daily prompts and reflections can be written by Gemini Nano, which runs on your phone. Your entries are not sent anywhere for this.
  • Claude (optional): if you add your own Anthropic API key, the weekly wrap-up and longer insights send the relevant entries to Anthropic's API to generate a response. Anthropic's privacy policy covers that processing. Nothing is sent to Claude unless you add a key.

Deleting your data

  • Entries: deleting an entry in the app deletes it everywhere it syncs.
  • Everything on the phone: uninstalling the app removes it all from the phone.
  • Synced data in Drive: go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Inkwell, and choose "Delete hidden app data".
  • Google access: you can revoke Inkwell's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on the inkwell-web GitHub repository.
